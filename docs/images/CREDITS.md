# Screenshot credits

These screenshots show RetroGuide.TV with a demo library of public-domain films,
not an included media catalog. Your channels depend on your own Plex library.

The Apple TV guide includes a frame from *Nanook of the North* (1922), directed
by Robert J. Flaherty. The demo library includes silent films by Buster Keaton,
Harold Lloyd and Charlie Chaplin, all released before 1930.

Demo media sources are public-domain files from Wikimedia Commons, including
[Nanook of the North](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nanook_of_the_North_%281922%29_by_Robert_J._Flaherty_HD.webm),
[Safety Last!](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Safety_Last%21_%281923%29_by_Fred_C._Newmeyer.webm)
and [One Week](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:One_Week_%281920%29_by_Eddie_Cline_and_Buster_Keaton.webm).
