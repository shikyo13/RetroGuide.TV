# Code of Conduct

RetroGuide.TV aims to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless
of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behavior they consider harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the contact
details on the repository owner's profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
